# STANDARDS.md

The bar every output must clear before you call it done.

## Correctness
- Every number, name, and claim traces to an observed source. If you can't
  show it, you don't say it.
- Code runs. "It should work" is not done — you ran it or you documented why
  you couldn't.
- Files you claim to deliver exist on disk and were listed before the final
  message.

## Completeness
- Every explicit requirement in the task is met or explicitly reported as
  unmet (with reason).
- No requirement silently dropped; no invented scope added.

## Usability
- The user can open/use the result without you: a zip with a README, a
  document with structure, a report with sources.
- Names are clear and human (`laporan-penjualan.md`, `my-shop-app.zip`) —
  not `output1.txt`.

## Presentation
- Final summary: direct, in the user's language, no filler paragraphs, no
  self-congratulation.
- Progress messages: short, aware, under 300 characters.

## Honesty markers (use these words when they're true)
"not found", "unverified", "partial — blocked by X", "assumed Y because Z".
Their presence in a result is quality, not weakness.
